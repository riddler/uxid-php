## Unreleased

The PHP port of uxid: prefixed, K-sortable identifiers a person can read,
wire-compatible with the Elixir `uxid` package. No version has been released
yet. The package is in place: the Composer package `riddler/uxid`, the
namespace `Riddler\Uxid` over `src/`, a PHP 8.3 floor and the gate. The
generation, decoding and validity checks arrive in later changes.
